# InsureCRM — Telecaller's Guide

Your day in the CRM, from the first call to the renewal. You only ever see your own leads,
customers and follow-ups.

## Start of the day: the Dashboard

The Dashboard shows today's leads, calls done, calls connected, follow-ups due (and how many
are overdue), interested customers, quotes sent, policies sold and how far you are towards
this month's target. Below that is **Today's priority leads**: work down it from the top.

If you are on leave or in training, press **I'm away**. You receive no new leads until you
press **I'm back**.

## Calling a lead

1. Press **Call** on a lead, from the Dashboard, the Leads list, Follow-ups or the customer's
   profile. Your phone dials the number, and the CRM times the call on screen.
2. When the call ends, choose what happened. Each outcome asks only for what it needs:

| Outcome | You must add | What the CRM does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Busy, no answer or switched off | — | Counts the attempt and books a retry for tomorrow; an interested customer stays interested |
| Asked to call back | Date (and time if agreed) | Books the follow-up |
| Interested | — | Sets the lead to *Interested* and creates "Send quotation" for today |
| Will send documents | — | Sets *Documents Pending* and books collection for tomorrow, naming what is missing |
| Not interested | A reason | Closes the lead |
| Wrong number | — | Takes the lead out of your calling list |
| Already insured elsewhere | Their current policy's expiry | Books a call 30 days before it expires |
| Bought the policy | Insurer, premium, dates | Creates the customer and the policy |

If you close the call screen without choosing, the call waits under the lead as *Record
outcome*.

## Follow-ups

**Follow-ups** has four tabs: Overdue, Due today, Upcoming and Done today. You are reminded before
a follow-up is due. If you miss one, you get an alert, and one left overdue is escalated to
your manager. Mark a follow-up done as soon as you have done it, because your on-time rate
counts.

## Quotations

On a lead, add a quotation with the insurer and premium. Move it on as the customer responds:
**Sent → Viewed → Negotiation → Accepted** (or Rejected). Sending a quote moves the lead to
*Quote Sent* and books a follow-up. Acceptance moves it to *Documents Pending* and lists the
documents still missing. A quote past its validity date expires by itself; send a revised one.

## Documents

Upload the customer's RC, driving licence, ID, previous policy or health documents on the
lead. The lead shows which required documents are still missing.

## Customers and renewals

After a sale, the customer's profile shows their whole history: calls, quotes, documents,
policies, follow-ups, notes and any earlier insurance. Renewals open by themselves 60 days
before a policy expires, and you are reminded at 60, 30, 15 and 7 days and on the due date.

## Your performance

**Performance** shows your calls, connect rate, conversions, premium and target progress. The
Dashboard shows this month's incentive estimate, which updates with every sale.
